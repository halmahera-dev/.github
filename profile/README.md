## What we build

- **Ask:** chat on your own data. "Why did sales drop in Surabaya last week?"
- **Read:** turn invoices, forms, and contracts into data.
- **Act:** automation that drafts the work and waits for your approval.
- **Alert:** early warnings from sensors and cameras.

Your data stays in your systems.
